Getting started

  1. Create an account with your email address and confirm it from the email we send.
  2. Fill in your name and your pickleball rating (your best guess is fine; MKRK adjusts it as you play).
  3. At the courts, scan the venue's QR code or enter the venue code your host gives you, then tap Check in.
  4. Tap Ready to play to join the line. When you're called to a court, tap On my way within a minute.

Common questions

Why can't I check in? You need to be at the courts, and MKRK needs permission to use your location once when you tap Check in. If GPS won't cooperate, a host can check you in.

I missed my call. You're moved to Taking a break. Tap Ready when you're back to rejoin the line.

The result was entered wrong. If the two teams report different winners, the game is marked disputed and a host settles it.

How do I run open play at my courts? Email us and we'll set up your venue.
